    Ambulances drive blind.

    Bed counts sit inside hospital records. Crews can't see them, so critical patients are driven to a full hospital and referred again. In an emergency, every minute counts.

    • No live visibility

      Bed counts sit in hospital records. Drivers cannot see them.

    • Phone-call guesswork

      Crews call or drive from hospital to hospital, losing minutes at each one.

    • Repeated re-referrals

      Patients reach a full hospital and are sent on again.

    • Uneven load

      Some hospitals overflow while nearby ones are under-used.

    From bed update to admission in four steps

    Scroll to follow the ambulance along the route.

    1. 1

      Staff update bed counts

      Hospital staff change emergency, ICU, ventilator and oxygen bed counts in one tap. Every update carries a timestamp.

      Hospital portal
    2. 2

      The cloud syncs instantly

      A central server stores the live counts and flags any hospital that has not updated for 30 minutes.

      Server and database
    3. 3

      The driver sees the nearest open hospital

      The app ranks hospitals by distance and ETA, hides nothing, and marks full ones clearly. One tap starts navigation.

      Driver app
    4. 4

      The driver pre-books and a token is issued

      A bed is held for 30 minutes. The family checks the token, the hospital confirms it, and the patient is admitted on arrival.

      Patient dashboard

    What each user gets, and what comes next

    Solid dots work in the demo today. Dashed dots are planned improvements.

    Patient family

    Works today

    • Check a token and see its status at a glance
    • See when the hospital last confirmed the bed
    • Ask the hospital to reconfirm

    Planned next

    • Token and alerts by SMS or WhatsApp, in Hindi and English
    • Live ambulance location
    • Minimal patient data, in line with the DPDP Act, 2023

    Ambulance driver

    Works today

    • Hospitals ranked by distance and ETA
    • One-tap navigation and pre-booking
    • Warnings when a hospital's data is stale

    Planned next

    • Road ETA with live traffic
    • Match by specialty: trauma, cardiac, stroke, neonatal
    • Offline cache, voice prompts and auto re-routing on decline

    Hospital staff

    Works today

    • One-tap bed counters with an updated-at time
    • Incoming tokens to confirm, decline or admit
    • Expired tokens release the bed automatically

    Planned next

    • Counts pulled from the hospital's own system
    • Staff logins with an audit log
    • Pre-arrival summary and peak-hour analytics
